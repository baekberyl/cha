WE'RE HIRING

Studio assistants, photo editors, and creative technologists.

Q: How long is each session?

A: Each station session is approximately 20 minutes. This includes setup time, shooting, and selecting your outputs. We recommend arriving 5 minutes early.

Q: Can I walk in without a reservation?

A: Yes, walk-ins are welcome but reservations are recommended, especially on weekends and evenings. Booking guarantees your preferred time slot.

Q: What should I wear?

A: Wear whatever makes you feel confident! For the Vintage Press Studio, classic or retro styling complements the aesthetic beautifully.
